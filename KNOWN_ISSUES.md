# Known Issues

This list is intentionally conservative. A feature is not marked as physically validated unless it has been tested on a real phone.

## Current known areas

- Orientation behavior still needs real-device validation.
- Camera/Screenshot flows are still being corrected and validated.
- Real two-finger multitouch over external games is not solved.
- Waydroid is mainly for visual inspection and does not replace phone testing.
- Compatibility depends on the game/app and device.
- Haptics may feel excessive in games that already use vibration heavily.
- Screenshot capture may require visible Android consent depending on system state/API rules.
- Alpha builds expire after 7 days once the tester build mechanism is active.

## Not yet promised

- Compatibility with every game.
- Invisible or automatic screenshot capture without Android consent.
- DRM-proof protection against modified APKs.
- Support for third-party executable plugins or scripts.
