# Code of Conduct

The AtherKeyring community is for constructive Alpha testing, compatibility feedback, ideas, and questions.

Please:

- be respectful;
- assume good faith;
- keep bug reports factual;
- avoid harassment, insults, or personal attacks;
- do not post private data;
- do not pressure testers to install unofficial APKs;
- do not share modified APKs as official builds.

Moderation may remove spam, unsafe links, harassment, or posts containing sensitive information.
